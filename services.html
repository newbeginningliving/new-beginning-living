Admission

Who We Serve

New Beginning Living Inc is a non-medical Residential Care Facility for the Elderly (RCFE) — also known as a board and care home. Admission is based on California RCFE guidelines and an individualized needs assessment.

  • Seniors generally 60 years of age or older
  • Does not require 24-hour skilled nursing or acute medical care
  • Able to be safely cared for in a non-medical, residential setting
  • Ambulatory or able to transfer safely, with or without a mobility aid
  • Free of behaviors that could pose a danger to self or others
  • Willing to participate in a shared, family-style living environment
  • Completed physician's report and pre-admission appraisal, per California Community Care Licensing requirements
  • Current TB clearance and required immunization records

Every situation is unique — if you're unsure whether your loved one qualifies, please call us. We're happy to talk through your specific needs.

Common Questions

RCFE & Board and Care Home FAQs

Answers to what families in San Diego most often ask about residential care and assisted living.

What is an RCFE (Residential Care Facility for the Elderly)?

An RCFE is a state-licensed home that provides non-medical care, supervision, and assistance with daily living for seniors who can no longer live safely on their own. In California, RCFEs are also commonly called board and care homes or assisted living homes.

What's the difference between a board and care home and assisted living?

Both terms describe RCFE-licensed senior care in California. Board and care homes, like New Beginning Living Inc, are typically smaller residential properties (often six beds or fewer) offering more personalized, family-style care, while "assisted living" often refers to larger communities with many more residents and a more institutional feel.

How do I know when it's time for residential care for a parent?

Common signs include difficulty with daily tasks like bathing, dressing, or managing medications; increased falls or safety concerns at home; social isolation; or caregiver burnout among family members. We're happy to talk through your specific situation and help you decide what's right for your family.

What are the admission requirements at New Beginning Living Inc?

Residents are generally 60 or older, do not require 24-hour skilled nursing care, and can be safely cared for in a non-medical, residential setting. See our full admission requirements above, or call us to discuss your loved one's specific needs.

What does it cost to live at New Beginning Living Inc?

Rates depend on the level of care each resident needs. Call or email us and we'll walk you through pricing and what's included in your loved one's individualized care plan.
