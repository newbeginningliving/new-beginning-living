Our Story

A True Sense of Family

New Beginning Living Inc is a Residential Care Facility for the Elderly (RCFE) — sometimes called a board and care home or assisted living home — founded on a simple belief: seniors deserve care that feels like home, not an institution. We provide compassionate, high-quality senior care in a warm, welcoming home in San Diego, CA, where every resident is known by name and treated with dignity.

Our residential care home is licensed by the California Department of Social Services, Community Care Licensing Division, to serve as a Residential Care Facility for the Elderly (RCFE) — giving families peace of mind that their loved ones are cared for by a qualified, regulated team.

Why It Matters

Why We Chose a Small Board and Care Home

Many large assisted living communities and senior care facilities house dozens, even hundreds, of residents. We chose to stay small — just six beds — on purpose. It means:

  • A lower caregiver-to-resident ratio
  • Individualized care plans, not one-size-fits-all routines
  • A quiet, residential neighborhood setting
  • Real relationships between residents, families, and staff
